🗺️ Top Sightseeing in Kanatal

🛕 Surkanda Devi Temple

Short trek or ropeway to this famous temple with stunning 360° Himalayan views. Spiritual and scenic.

🌲 Kaudia Forest

Dense deodar forest perfect for birdwatching, photography, and nature walks. Jeep safari available.

🌿 Eco Park

Well-maintained park with Himalayan views, walking trails, and picnic spots. Great for families.

🏞️ Tehri Lake

Asia's largest man-made reservoir. Water sports, boating, and breathtaking views of the submerged old Tehri town.

🏚️ Heritage Village Walk

Walk through 100-year-old Pahadi heritage villages. Terraced fields, mud houses, and authentic mountain life.

🏔️ Himalayan Viewpoints

Multiple sunrise and sunset points with unobstructed views of Bandarpunch, Swargarohini, and Gangotri ranges.

📍 How to Reach Kanatal

By Road: Kanatal is ~300 km from Delhi (7-8 hours via Chamba-Mussoorie Road). Drive or take a bus to Chamba/Rishikesh, then a local taxi.

By Air: Nearest airport is Dehradun (Jolly Grant, 90 km). We provide airport pickup in Dzire/Innova.

By Train: Nearest railway station is Dehradun (85 km) or Rishikesh (75 km).

Best Time: March-June (summer, 10-25°C) | October-February (winter, snow possible) | July-September (monsoon, less crowded).
